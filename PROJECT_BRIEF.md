# Project brief

## Purpose and audience

Help clients, agencies, and employers understand what Josri Ocaña builds. Position Josri as a GoHighLevel Automation Specialist & CRM Systems Builder based in Butuan City, Philippines, with approximately two years of hands-on GoHighLevel experience in agency-managed and client-owned accounts.

The primary asset is an expandable library of real work: CRM systems, workflows, websites, funnels, Make.com scenarios, integrations, and lead/appointment flows. Screenshots, architecture, walkthroughs, and verified results should provide the evidence.

## Visual and interaction direction

Editorial minimalism with cinematic interaction. White and warm white surfaces, near-black typography, restrained rules, and occasional inverted sections. Barlow Condensed supplies the large architectural headings; Manrope supports readable UI and copy. No blue, gradients, invented statistics, testimonials, or stock client stories.

The supplied descriptions of Guillaume Zhu and Numa inform the motion mechanics: expressive line reveals and structured pinned assembly, reinterpreted as an original CRM diagram. No reference assets, source code, branding, or exact compositions are used.

The rhythm alternates calm editorial reading with purposeful motion. Desktop can pin the connected system. Mobile uses a normal-flow grid. Reduced-motion users retain all information without pinning or reveal animation.

## Information architecture

Navigation → typographic hero → services → connected automation story → selected work → workflow explanation → websites/funnels → six-stage process → grouped tools → concise about → FAQ → contact → footer.

`/work` is the filterable project library. `/work/[slug]` provides reusable case studies with optional media, architecture, results, and links. Empty states are honest while verified project material is being prepared.

## Content and discoverability

Use natural, explicit terminology: GoHighLevel, CRM setup, workflow automation, lead routing, funnels, Make.com, APIs, and webhooks. Concise service definitions, FAQs, semantic headings, server-rendered project content, and factual structured data help people and answer engines understand the work. No promised search volumes or rankings.

Keep content centrally editable and separate from layout. Contact details remain unconfigured until a verified address is supplied. Publish only factual project results and approved client information.

## Outcome

A maintainable, accessible, responsive portfolio ready for GitHub and Vercel, with reusable project/media architecture, SEO fundamentals, deliberate motion, and documentation that supports future local changes.
